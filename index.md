## About me

Hi there! I'm Zhengqing, an interdisciplinary researcher in my 4th year of PhD at [You Lab](https://www.youlab.bio/), Duke University. I work on the predictive modeling of biological time-series, with microbiome as my current system of interest.

A physicist and biomedical engineer by training, I strive to bridge theory, computation, and experiment together, with the ultimate goal of translating quantitative insights into biomedical applications. I believe we can derive elegant principles to engineer biology the same way we build vehicles, planes, and rockets. Common tools that I use include: representation & supervised learning, systems biology & dynamical modeling, and sequencing-based high-throughput experiments.
